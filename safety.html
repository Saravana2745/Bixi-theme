Safety, Care & Damage Policy

Your safety across Madurai streets and Tamil Nadu highways comes first. Review our mandatory rider safety rules, KYC verification, and vehicle care guidelines at BIXI RIDE Madurai.

01

Personal Safety & Rider Responsibility (Madurai Operations)

  • Personal Safety: The rider is completely responsible for his or her personal safety. Once the rental agreement and deal are finalised with BIXI RIDE Madurai, the rider operates the vehicle at their own risk.
  • Mandatory Licence Submission: The customer is required to present their original valid Driving Licence for verification and submit a clear Xerox / digital copy of their licence and Government ID proof for all car and two-wheeler rentals in Madurai.
  • Mandatory Helmets & Seat Belts: ISI-certified helmets are strictly compulsory for both rider and pillion on all two-wheelers, and seat belts are compulsory for all car occupants across Madurai and surrounding highways.
  • Zero Intoxication & Traffic Compliance: Drinking and driving, or use of any intoxicants while riding or driving, is strictly against BIXI RIDE company policy. Every rider and driver must abide by all rules and regulations of the Motor Vehicles Department of the Government of India and Tamil Nadu Traffic Police.
  • Traffic Fines Responsibility: Any fines issued for riding without a helmet, over-speeding, signal jumping, wrong-route entry, or unauthorized parking during your Madurai trip are 100% the customer's responsibility.
  • Unforeseen & Condition Clause: Damage to the vehicle may be reviewed only under extraordinary, unforeseen circumstances as assessed by BIXI RIDE Madurai management. Every vehicle must be returned in sound, safe, and roadworthy condition (the exact condition in which it was handed over at our Madurai hub).
02

Damage and Loss Policy

  • Pre-Ride Inspection: Every bike and car shall be jointly inspected by the BIXI RIDE Madurai team and the rider/driver before handover (with clear timestamped photo/video inspection). The customer will be liable for any damages, scratches, dents, or losses caused thereafter.
  • Internal Mechanical Servicing: Any internal service-related mechanical issue or routine breakdown within Madurai city limits will be promptly handled directly by our BIXI RIDE Madurai technical support and service hubs (Mattuthavani & Madurai Main).
  • Condition at Return: The rented vehicle must be returned back to BIXI RIDE Madurai in the exact same condition as it was handed over at the time of renting.
  • Repair & Replacement Liability: You are responsible to pay the actual repair charges, spare replacement costs, or any other applicable charges if there is any scratch, dent, accident damage, or loss to the rented vehicle during your trip.
  • Handover Compliance: Once the rider complies with all BIXI RIDE Terms & Conditions and KYC checks, the sanitized vehicle is safely handed over for usage.
  • Theft & Negligence Responsibility: By accepting the terms and conditions, the rider promises to take due care of the rented vehicle as if the vehicle belongs to them. Any damage, theft, or loss caused by negligence, carelessness, improper parking, or unauthorized handover has to be made good by the customer. BIXI RIDE Madurai shall not be liable for any unlawful acts committed by the user during the rented period with the rented vehicle.
