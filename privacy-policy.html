Privacy Policy

At BIXI RIDE – Car & Bike Rental (Madurai), safeguarding your personal identity documents, booking records, and digital payment details is our utmost priority.

Terms & Conditions → Safety Guidelines →
01

Information We Collect

  • Contact & Booking Details: Full name, mobile/WhatsApp number, pickup/drop-off dates, pickup location in Madurai (Mattuthavani, Railway Junction, or Google Location doorstep address), and selected vehicle model.
  • KYC & Government ID Verification for Vehicle Safety: We collect and verify your Valid Driving Licence and Original Government-Issued Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID) strictly for our bike and car safety. Because BIXI Ride operates on a Zero Security Deposit policy, verified Government ID proof protects our fleet against vehicle theft, unauthorized third-party handovers, reckless misuse, and ensures full compliance with Tamil Nadu Motor Vehicles Department (RTO) and Madurai Police safety rules.
  • Payment & Transaction Metadata: Booking reference IDs and Razorpay/WooCommerce payment confirmation tokens (e.g. `razorpay_payment_id`). We do not store your credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs on our servers.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To reserve your selected bike or car in real time and communicate handover timing or booking extensions.
  • For Bike & Car Safety Protection: To confirm rider identity before handing over keys with zero security deposit, prevent vehicle theft or misuse, and ensure accountability for any traffic fines, accidents, or vehicle damages during the rental period.
  • To verify driver eligibility and maintain statutory commercial vehicle rental registers in Madurai.
  • To verify advance payments securely via Razorpay / WooCommerce and issue instant digital booking confirmations.
  • To coordinate 24/7 roadside assistance, traffic violation notifications, or insurance processing if required by law.
03

Secure Razorpay Payment Processing

  • All online booking advance payments (₹300 for bikes / ₹500 for cars) are processed exclusively over encrypted SSL/TLS connections through PCI-DSS compliant Razorpay & WooCommerce gateways.
  • Payment signature verification happens server-side to protect against unauthorized tampering or fraudulent transactions.
04

Data Sharing & Strict Confidentiality

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details or identity proofs to third-party marketers or spam lists.
  • Information is shared strictly when legally mandated by Tamil Nadu Police, Motor Vehicles Department (RTO), traffic enforcement authorities (e-Challan verification), or our insurance partners during an active claim investigation.
05

Cookies, Analytics & Retention

  • Essential browser storage is used solely to remember your selected booking dates, vehicle filter preferences, and smooth UI animations.
  • Rental records and KYC logs are securely retained only for the duration required by Indian transport regulations and tax accounting laws.
06

Your Rights & Grievance Contact

  • You may request review, correction, or deletion of your non-statutory contact data by emailing info@bixiride.com or calling our Madurai administrative desk.
  • Updates to this Privacy Policy will be published directly on this page with immediate effect.
