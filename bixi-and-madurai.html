SACRED HILL TEMPLE 21 km • 35 mins

Alagar Kovil (Kallazhagar Temple)

Route: K. Pudur → Alagar Kovil Main Road Best Time: Morning 6:00 AM – 11:30 AM & Evening 4:30 PM

2,500-Year Ancient Legacy & Cultural Heritage

Nestled amidst the lush forested slopes of the Alagar Hills (Alagar Malai), Alagar Kovil is an ancient Divya Desam dedicated to Lord Sundararaja Perumal (Kallazhagar). Celebrated in Sangam literature and celebrated during the world-renowned Chithirai Festival, millions of devotees gather as Lord Kallazhagar, riding a golden horse chariot, journeys toward the sacred Vaigai river. Higher up the mountain slopes lies the holy spring of Noopura Gangai (Rakkayi Amman Kovil), where natural sweet mountain water has cascaded without interruption for millennia.
