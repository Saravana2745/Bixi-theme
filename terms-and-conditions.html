Booking & Payment

02

Documents & Eligibility

  • Customers must provide a valid driving licence appropriate for the rented vehicle and a valid government-issued identity proof.

  • Only the customer named in the rental agreement is permitted to drive the vehicle unless another driver is expressly approved by BIXI RIDE.
03

Vehicle Collection & Return

  • The vehicle must be collected and returned at the agreed location and time.
  • Customers must return the vehicle in the same condition as received, allowing for normal wear and tear.

  • Late returns may attract additional rental charges as communicated at the time of booking.

Fuel & Rental Charges

  • The vehicle must be returned with the same fuel level as provided by BIXI RIDE at the time of delivery.

  • Customers are responsible for refueling the vehicle at their own expense during the rental period.
  • Tolls, parking fees and other trip-related expenses are the customer's responsibility.
  • Additional rental charges may apply for extra usage, late return or other services agreed upon during booking.
05

Vehicle Safety & Usage

  • Customers must obey all traffic rules and drive responsibly.
  • Wearing a helmet is compulsory for two-wheeler riders, and wearing a seat belt is compulsory for car occupants, as applicable.
  • Drunk driving, reckless driving, racing, illegal activities and misuse of the vehicle are strictly prohibited.
  • The vehicle must not be used for unauthorized commercial activities, sub-rental or any unlawful purpose.
  • Customers must not allow unauthorized persons to operate the vehicle.
06

Accidents, Damage, Insurance & Breakdown

  • Customers must inspect the vehicle and report any existing damage before starting the trip.
  • Any accident, collision, vehicle damage, theft or mechanical problem must be reported to BIXI RIDE immediately.
  • The customer shall be responsible for any loss or damage arising from an accident, theft, collision, scratches, dents or any other damage to the rented vehicle during the rental period, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
  • If an insurance claim is rejected, denied, not approved or not fully covered by the insurance company for any reason, the customer shall be responsible for the full cost of repairs, replacement, vehicle loss, insurance excess and any other related expenses, to the extent legally recoverable.
  • Any amount approved or paid by the insurance company shall be adjusted against the total loss or repair cost. The customer shall be responsible for the remaining amount not covered by insurance, where legally recoverable.
  • Customers must immediately inform BIXI RIDE of any accident, theft or damage and cooperate with the insurance company and relevant authorities.
  • Customers must not arrange repairs or abandon the vehicle without informing BIXI RIDE, except where immediate action is necessary for safety.
  • All damage charges shall be assessed based on the actual repair or replacement cost and supported by appropriate documentation.
07

Traffic Fines & Legal Responsibilities

  • Customers are responsible for traffic fines, penalties, toll charges and other violations incurred during their rental period.
  • Customers must cooperate with BIXI RIDE and the authorities in case of an accident, traffic violation or legal inquiry relating to the rental period.

Security Deposit

  • Any applicable security deposit will be communicated before booking.
  • The deposit will be refunded after vehicle inspection and settlement of outstanding charges.
  • Any deductions for damage, fines, unpaid rental charges or other agreed charges will be explained to the customer.

  • All booking advances are 100% non-refundable.
